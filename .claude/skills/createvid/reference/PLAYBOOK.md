# Cinematic Launch Video Playbook

This playbook is for music launch videos built from stills, an MP3 and type. It is written for the artist's project, and its goal is work that reads like a senior editor cut it.

---

## 0. The rule: plan before every task

Before rendering anything for a request, write a **Shot Plan** (template in §9). It must cover:
- which tools, skills and repos each shot uses, and why;
- the beat map from `beats.py`;
- the grade;
- the type system;
- the deliverables.

Nothing is rendered until the plan is written.

If the artist supplies an **MP3/WAV, it is the soundtrack. Always.** Run `tools/beats.py` on it first, because every cut decision comes from that file.

---

## 1. What separates "senior editor" from "AI slop"

| Slop tell | What we do instead |
|---|---|
| Every shot the same length and the same Ken Burns zoom | Cut on the **beat grid**, but vary the phrase lengths (4-4-2-2-1-1-**hold**). Every move is chosen: push, orbit, rise or dolly-zoom, each with a reason |
| Random transitions (spins, page curls, glitch everywhere) | **Hard cuts** about 90% of the time. Transitions are *spent*: one flash on the drop, blur-cuts inside a phrase, a whip only to change act |
| Motion that "breathes" or wobbles in place | Motion **performs**: it starts mid-move, ends mid-move and carries momentum across the cut (*hf-motion-doctrine*, vector law) |
| Default fonts, centred, five sizes, drop shadows | One serif display face and one grotesk. Two sizes. Wide tracking on small caps. Text sits in negative space |
| Over-saturated, crunchy HDR | One coherent **grade** for the whole film (LUT, halation, grain, vignette), with skin protected |
| Text over busy detail | Titles land on **dark or empty frames** or on a cutout layer (text *behind* the subject) |
| Constant energy | **Contrast**: stillness before the drop, then release. Pause one frame of black before the hook |
| Every frame in focus | Depth of field, rack focus, foreground occlusion |
| The music only plays underneath | The **music drives the edit**: cut points, speed ramps and flashes all come from `beats.json` |

## 2. Structure for a 15–30s launch teaser (9:16 first)

1. **Cold open (0–2s).** One striking frame. Slow push with DOF, almost silent (a reverse swell or riser from `sfx/`). No text yet.
2. **Build (2s → drop).** Cuts every 2 bars, then every bar, then every beat as the riser climbs. Micro type fragments: one word at a time.
3. **Drop.** A white flash or a 1-frame black, a hard cut to the hero shot (best photo, dolly-zoom or orbit) and an `impact_cinematic.wav`.
4. **Hook (after the drop).** Fastest cutting, on beats and strong onsets. Cutout layers. Optionally text behind the subject.
5. **Title card.** Artist name with collapsing tracking, then the song title as a mask-reveal serif, then a rule line, then the date (Remotion `Title`).
6. **Button.** End on the cover art or a still. The date stays on screen for at least 1.5s. Hard out on a downbeat.

For a 30–60s cut, repeat the build and drop once, using different shots.

## 3. Turning stills into footage (the core problem)

- **Depth parallax.** `tools/parallax.py`, or DepthFlow for GL-shader looks.
  - Moves: `push`, `pull`, `orbit`, `pan-left/right`, `rise/fall`, `dolly-zoom`. Strength 0.6–1.2; `--dof 0.4–0.8` for portraits.
  - Faces: keep the push strength at 1.0 or below. Prefer orbit or rise for close-ups.
- **Layer separation.** `tools/cutout.py`:
  - gives the subject as RGBA, plus the background plate;
  - animate each layer separately, with the background blurred and slower;
  - this is also how you put text **behind** her.
- **Speed ramps.** Run a parallax clip at 1.0 speed into the cut, then ease to 0.3 on the drop.
- **Micro-motion over static.** Never let a still sit still for more than 12 frames, unless the stillness is deliberate before a climax.
- **Upscale weak photos** before anything else: Lanczos is the CPU default. Real-ESRGAN needs a GPU (see *cv-video-enhance*).
- **Crop with intent.** 9:16 from a landscape photo loses a lot. Use `--w/--h` and pick a focal point. Or use the landscape photo as a background plate behind a cutout.

## 4. Colour: one look per film

Run `python tools/grade.py IN OUT --preset film|music|clean|noir`, or set options individually. The LUTs in `luts/` are generated by `tools/gen_luts.py`, which makes original looks with no licensing issues:

| LUT | Use for |
|---|---|
| `print_film` | default cinematic: warm print, dense blacks |
| `teal_orange` | big, blockbuster, energetic pop/EDM |
| `neon_split` | night, club, alt-pop, R&B with attitude |
| `pastel_dream` | soft, romantic, bedroom pop |
| `midnight_blue` | moody, sad, late night |
| `golden_hour` | warm, nostalgic, acoustic |
| `kodak_portrait` | clean beauty, skin-first |
| `faded_matte` | editorial, fashion, indie |
| `bleach_bypass` | gritty, rock, intense |
| `gritty_street` | rap/drill |
| `noir_mono` | B&W statements, or a single act change |

Grading rules:
- Grade **once at the end** over the whole edit, so every shot shares one look.
- Change the look only at an **act change**.
- Check skin on a 100% crop. Never judge a grade from a downscaled montage: ImageMagick's downscaled previews add fake banding.
- To try looks side by side, use `hyperframes grade-compare`, or render stills with several presets.

## 5. Typography

Fonts are in `fonts/` (local, installed to `~/.fonts`). In Remotion, load them through `@remotion/google-fonts/<Name>`.

- **Serif display:** Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display, DM Serif Display, Instrument Serif, Italiana, Cormorant Garamond, Fraunces, Cinzel.
- **Grotesk/sans:** Inter Tight, Space Grotesk, Manrope, Archivo, Outfit, Syne, Unbounded, Montserrat.
- **Condensed/impact:** Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald, Big Shoulders Display, Six Caps.
- **Mono/accents:** JetBrains Mono, Space Mono. Monoton for retro.

Rules:
- At most 2 families.
- Use expo-out eases (`bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`) for arrivals and expo-in for exits.
- Mask reveals beat fades.
- Stagger words by 0.08–0.15s (the waterfall in *hf-cut-the-curve*).
- Keep text off the outer 8% of the frame, and clear of the bottom 20% in 9:16 (that area is covered by the TikTok/Reels UI).

## 6. Sound design (even though the song is the star)

Files in `sfx/`:
- `riser_4s` / `riser_8s`: end exactly on the drop.
- `reverse_swell`: into the first cut.
- `impact_cinematic` + `sub_drop`: on the drop and on title hits.
- `whoosh_fast` / `whoosh_slow`: under whip moves only.
- `click_shutter`: on photo-flash cuts.
- `tape_hiss_loop`: very low, for texture in quiet intros.

Mix SFX about 10–14 dB under the song. Master to −14 LUFS for streaming platforms (*cv-video-audio*).

## 7. Toolbelt: what to reach for

| Need | Tool / skill |
|---|---|
| Analyse the song | `tools/beats.py` (librosa). Also `hyperframes beats` / `hyperframes audio` |

## 8. Deliverables

| Format | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Reels/TikTok/Shorts (master) |
| 4:5 | 1080×1350 | IG feed |
| 1:1 | 1080×1080 | feed/cover loops |
| 16:9 | 1920×1080 (or 3840×2160) | YouTube/premiere |

Export settings:
- Video: H.264 High, CRF 14–16, `-tune grain`, yuv420p, 30 fps (or 24 for a filmic feel).
- Audio: AAC 320k.
- Keep a ProRes 4444 master of every title and overlay.
